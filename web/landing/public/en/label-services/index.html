01

How can I manage releases for several artists?

AUDENIQ is developing a catalog management system that shows tracks and artist information, release schedules and distribution status in one place. It is meant both for an individual’s first release and for labels running releases for several artists.

The plan is for teams to coordinate their work together, with only users who hold the necessary permissions able to access information.

02

How can I check revenue and per-rights-holder payouts?

AUDENIQ is preparing a system that keeps platform revenue and confirmed payout statements apart so both are easy to check. It is also being developed to manage payout details for every rights holder you work with.

Monthly payouts and Korean-language customer support are in preparation, and actual payouts will follow the music services’ revenue reports and payment confirmation.

03

What support is planned after a release?

AUDENIQ is preparing artist profile linking, delivery of the lyrics you provide, and tools to announce a release such as Smart Link, Pre-save, Pre-order and promo cards. Profile issue support, music video releases and rights management for music used in online videos are also being prepared as options where needed.

Later, AUDENIQ plans to expand toward connecting artists, songwriters, lyricists, producers and other labels for collaboration. Timing and platform-specific support will be announced at launch.

Can small labels use AUDENIQ?＋

Yes. AUDENIQ is preparing its services for independent labels, including small labels, as well as individual artists.

Do solo artists need to use the label management service?＋

No. Individual artists will be able to use the services they need for their own releases and distribution. Services built around label team operations are designed to be chosen only when needed.

Are rights management and publishing included automatically?＋

No. Publishing is a separate service chosen and contracted after checking the rights status of each work. The actual scope of distribution and label support will also each be announced at launch.
