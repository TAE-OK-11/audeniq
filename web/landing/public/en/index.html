What is AUDENIQ (어데닉)?＋

AUDENIQ (Korean name: 어데닉) is a music platform for independent artists, songwriters and labels that is preparing to launch in South Korea. Built around music distribution in Korea and internationally, it is preparing services that connect music publishing, catalog and revenue management, and release support.

Is AUDENIQ a music distributor?＋

AUDENIQ is preparing a music distribution service, but it has not started distributing music yet. It is currently preparing to incorporate and to sign contracts with music services (DSPs) in Korea and abroad, and plans to offer music distribution and music publishing as separate services.

Can I release music or sign up for publishing with AUDENIQ now?＋

Not yet. AUDENIQ is preparing to launch its distribution system and publishing service, and will announce when applications open and what each service supports on its official website and social channels.

Which music platforms can AUDENIQ distribute to?＋

Supported music platforms have not been confirmed yet. AUDENIQ is prioritizing direct contracts with major Korean music services and plans to broaden its reach through direct international contracts and Merlin membership and licensing. Supported platforms and countries will be announced once contracts are confirmed.

What is the difference between music distribution and music publishing?＋

Music distribution delivers recorded music to music services and pays out the revenue from its use. Music publishing is a separate service that administers royalties for the compositions and lyrics behind a song and pitches them for new uses. AUDENIQ will explain the contract and payout scope of each separately.

What is AUDENIQ Publishing, and who can use it?＋

AUDENIQ Publishing is a music publishing service in preparation that will help with registering compositions and lyrics, administering royalties and pitching songs for new uses. It is aimed at indie composers and lyricists, artists who write their own songs and freelance creators; the manageable scope will be set after reviewing existing collecting society or publishing agreements and the rights of each work.

If AUDENIQ distributes my music, do my rights transfer to AUDENIQ?＋

No. The standard distribution agreement is being prepared on a non-exclusive basis, and rights to recordings and works stay with the rights holders. AUDENIQ receives only the permissions needed for distribution, within the scope of the agreement. Publishing is also a separate service you choose and contract for.

Can I move music I have already released?＋

AUDENIQ is preparing distributor transfer support that keeps existing ISRCs, UPCs and release information. It plans to help you confirm the release and matching status on the new distribution route before ending the previous distributor’s delivery. Whether and how a transfer works depends on existing contracts and each platform’s policies.

Can both indie artists and independent labels use AUDENIQ?＋

Yes. AUDENIQ is preparing a service for everyone from an indie artist’s first release to independent labels managing many artists. For labels, it is developing tools to manage catalogs, release schedules and royalty accounting as a team.

Does AUDENIQ handle music videos and music used in online videos?＋

AUDENIQ is preparing support for the Korean music video rating process and for releasing music videos on global video platforms. Any required rating will go through the external rating body’s procedure. Rights management for music used in online videos, such as YouTube Content ID, is being prepared separately, and support depends on each platform’s and each piece of content’s rights requirements.

Will support and payouts be available in Korean?＋

AUDENIQ is preparing Korean-language customer support and a monthly payout system for users in Korea. Statements will follow the music services’ revenue reports and payout confirmation schedules.

Does taking the pre-launch survey sign me up?＋

No. The current survey only collects feedback for service planning. Taking it does not create an account or a distribution or publishing agreement.
